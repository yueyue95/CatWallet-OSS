# CatWallet v0.3.0

## English

CatWallet v0.3.0 expands the public ledger model for reimbursements and installment lifecycles.

- Record a reimbursement from the transaction experience and link it to the original expense.
- Support partial and multi-party reimbursements across receiving accounts while preserving the original gross expense.
- Keep reimbursements out of ordinary income and report the personal share after reimbursement.
- Include future installment occurrences in planned reserves without recognizing them early as spending or card liability.
- Preview, soft-delete, restore, and inspect archived installment groups as one atomic lifecycle, including linked transactions.
- Hide archived plans from the default view and preserve their real final occurrence dates.
- Expose the same reimbursement and installment lifecycle boundaries through the MCP capability catalog.

All examples and automated fixtures in this release use synthetic local data. No hosted account, private financial record, or deployment configuration is included.

## 简体中文

CatWallet v0.3.0 扩展了公开版的报销和分期生命周期能力。

- 可在交易流程中登记报销，并关联原始支出。
- 支持不同收款账户的部分及多人报销，同时保留原始订单总额。
- 报销回款不计入普通收入，报告按扣除报销后的个人承担统计。
- 未来分期期数计入计划预留，但不会提前成为消费或信用卡负债。
- 可按组预览、软删除、恢复和查看已归档分期；关联交易与分期组保持原子一致。
- 默认列表隐藏已归档计划，并保留其真实最后一期日期。
- MCP 能力目录同步公开相同的报销与分期生命周期边界。

本版本的所有示例与自动化 fixture 均使用本地合成数据，不包含托管账号、私人财务记录或部署配置。
